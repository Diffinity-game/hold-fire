# PARADIGM SHIFT

A vertical shooter that looks like 1986.

Kill everything. Grow stronger. That's how you win a shooter. Right?

Every kill makes you stronger — and the boss's armor thicker.
Your CHARGE grows while you stay silent. Why would it?

One file. No assets. Open `index.html` and play in the browser.
Touch: drag to move, tap FIRE to shoot. Keyboard: WASD/arrows + Z/J.

Please don't spoil the trick in the comments.
